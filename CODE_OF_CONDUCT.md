# Code of conduct

Chamoji follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Be kind and respectful,** in issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere else the project lives.
- **Assume good intent,** and welcome people of every background and experience level.
- **Give and take criticism about the work,** not the person.
- **No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or unwanted attention.**

**Reporting:** if someone breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainers through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form](../../security/advisories/new), or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block people who break the code.
